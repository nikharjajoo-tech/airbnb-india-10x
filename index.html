Four buckets: who travels, and where

Each booking falls in exactly one bucket

① Indians abroad

+₹0.50B

Lead here. One trip abroad is worth 4–5 trips at home, and it uses homes Airbnb already has.

  • Campaigns for family and friend groups to Dubai, Bali, Vietnam, Japan and Europe
  • Boutique hotels where homes are restricted (Thailand, Singapore)
  • Rupee pricing, visa-ready confirmations, kitchens for veg families
Target: ~1% of Indian leisure trips abroad (~0.16% today)

② Indians in India

+₹0.47B

Weekend and holiday getaways for groups from the top 8 metros.

  • Verified homes in 3–4 corridors first (Lonavala, Coorg, Himachal, Goa)
  • Villa brands list on Airbnb for quality group supply
  • Brand spend only after trust targets are met
Target: ~1.5% of India's homestay and villa bookings

③ The world in India

+₹0.09B

Foreign tourists and NRIs visiting home. Kept light on purpose.

  • Foreign arrivals are still below 2019
  • Form C filing helper so hosts accept foreign guests
Target: inbound revenue ~4x

④ Beyond stays

+₹0.25B

New revenue lines Airbnb is already building globally.

  • Experiences and Services in 5 Indian cities
  • Boutique and independent hotels in India
Target: ₹0.25B from zero

We stop the extra spend, never Airbnb India.

  1. Feature hurts conversion → switch it off
  2. Bet misses its target → end it, move the budget
  3. Group wedge fails (lift under 5%) → abroad only
  4. Payback over 18 months at two yearly gates → withhold next tranche

Two readings in a row, judged against control cities. Trust and safety commitments are never rolled back.

Not doing: weddings, package tours, budget rooms, fee increases, selling through MakeMyTrip.

A note on the baseline

This memo uses the brief's ₹250M. A listing-based estimate suggests Airbnb's India marketplace revenue may be 5–30x higher; ₹250M is close to the Indian entity's reported service revenue. The plan is built on ratios, so it holds either way. If the true base is larger, I would recommend a ~5x target.
